What is new: the app opens on "Сегодня" with a session of about six exercises, a count of
days of practice, a soft streak with freezes, and progress. Failed puzzles come back on an
FSRS schedule. "Ещё" holds settings (daily norm, calendar reminder), the data screen, the free
board and the diagnostics. One new trap a day.

If the phone still shows the old app, wait for the banner "Доступна новая версия" and press
"Обновить". "Ещё" then "Диагностика" must show "Версия" 0.6.0 and "Сборка" `m5 @`.

The preview has its own storage: it starts empty and does not touch the main site.

### What to judge by feel (please write a line about each)

- Is a session of six exercises the right size for five minutes?
- The order: warm-up, what is due, the trap, new puzzles from easy to hard. Would you change it?
- "Дней занятий" is the big number and the streak is the small one. Right that way round?
- One new trap a day as a hard rule: helpful, or in the way?
- The home screen: is anything missing that you look for first?
