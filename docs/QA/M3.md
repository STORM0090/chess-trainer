What is new: progress is saved on the device. A trap is learned in a backward chain (the last
move first, then the last two, back to move 1), reviewed on later days, and only then the next
trap opens. The list shows the status of every trap. The screen "Данные" exports and imports
the progress file.

If the phone still shows the old app, wait for the banner "Доступна новая версия" and press
"Обновить". "Данные" then "Диагностика устройства" must show "Версия" 0.4.0 and "Сборка" `m3 @`.

The preview has its own storage: it starts empty and does not touch the main site.

### What to judge by feel (please write a line about each)

- The chain goes one move of yours at a time from the end. Is that the "откат на ход назад"
  you had in mind, or should the first step be the whole blow at once?
- Is one clean pass per step the right bar, or too strict (too lax)?
- Deviations come after the step they belong to and can be skipped. Useful, or in the way?
- The next trap opens only after a review on another day. Is one new trap in about two days
  the pace you want?
- The three unanswered M2 questions are still open: hint texts and order, the 0.65 s pause
  before the opponent's move, and the number of variants per trap.

## Known limits of this stage

- The order inside a stage comes from the `order` field of the trap files. The owner confirmed
  the order on 2026-11-14; the list is in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, "Order inside a stage".
- "Я это знаю" skips one open trap at a time. The entry test that picks the starting stage
  is part of M4.
- Import replaces everything on the device; it does not merge two devices.
- The daily limit "one new trap a day" is not a separate rule yet. It follows from the review
  on the next day. The daily session with its own limit is M5.
- The game log only counts. The prompt to reconsider the Englund Gambit after 25 games is a
  line of text on doubtful traps, nothing more.
- Explanations under the lines are still the general ones from M2.
- A review picks its line at random (the main line half of the time), so two reviews of the
  same trap can look different.
- Checked by the developer in desktop Chrome at phone size and by two Playwright tests
  (headless Chromium, 384 x 824, touch events) that cover A1 to A8, A11, A15 to A19, A23,
  A24, A26, A27, A29 to A31 and A32 to A35. Not checked on a real phone: where Samsung Internet saves the
  file, the Android file picker, the installed PWA, and whether Android clears the storage of
  a site that is not installed.
