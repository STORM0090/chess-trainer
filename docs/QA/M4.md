What is new: a fourth header link, "Задачи". It has puzzles from the Lichess database in nine
themes, an entry test of ten puzzles that sets the level, and the same hints as the traps.
Puzzle progress is saved on the device and is part of the progress file.

If the phone still shows the old app, wait for the banner "Доступна новая версия" and press
"Обновить". "Данные" then "Диагностика устройства" must show "Версия" 0.5.0 and "Сборка" `m4 @`.

The preview has its own storage: it starts empty and does not touch the main site.

### What to judge by feel (please write a line about each)

- The level after the test: do the puzzles of your level feel right, too easy or too hard?
- The test counts a puzzle only without errors and hints, and keeps hints available. Right, or
  should a test puzzle end at the first wrong move?
- The theme names and one-line tasks: are "Незащищённая фигура", "Сквозной удар",
  "Вскрытое нападение" and "Отвлечение" the words you expect?
- A level goes up after 30 puzzles of the level with 70% solved cleanly. Too slow?
- The task line names the theme, so it is a hint in itself. Keep it, or hide the theme?
