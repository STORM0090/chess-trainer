What is new: "Игра с ботом" on the home screen and under "Ещё". Thirteen levels; the next
level is offered by your results. On the page of any open trap, "Сыграть с ботом" starts a
game in which the bot opens along the lines of the trap. An optional ШВУ checklist before
every move (settings). After a game the engine shows up to three of your biggest mistakes.

"Ещё" then "Диагностика" must show "Версия" 0.7.0 and "Сборка" `m6 @`.

## A. Samsung S24 Ultra

### First game (with the network on)

| # | Step | Expected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | Open "Сегодня". | A block "Игра с ботом": "Короткая партия против бота. Ваш уровень бота: 1." and a button "Сыграть". |
| A2 | Press "Сыграть". | "Загружаем шахматный движок…" for a moment, then the setup: "Уровень 1 из 13" with − and +, the colour (Белые / Чёрные / Случайно), the checklist switch, "Начать партию". |
| A3 | Choose "Белые", press "Начать партию". | A full-width board, "Вы играете белыми. Уровень 1 из 13", "Ваш ход". |
| A4 | Play a whole game. | The bot answers in about a second ("Бот думает…" in between). Its moves look like a beginner's: it takes what it can, sometimes leaves a piece hanging, sometimes misses your threat, but it does not play random moves. |
| A5 | Finish the game (or press "Сдаться"). | The result ("Победа!", "Ничья." or "Партия проиграна.") and the level for the next game: one up after a win, the same after one loss, one down after two losses in a row. |
| A6 | Press "Разбор: главные ошибки" (shown after a game with at least three of your moves). | "Движок смотрит партию: N из M…" for a few seconds, then up to three of your moves as buttons. Each shows the position before the move with the engine's move as an arrow, and "Вы сыграли …. Сильнее было …". Or "Крупных ошибок движок не нашёл." |
| A7 | Press "Ещё партия" a few times; win and lose. | The level follows the results. "Сегодня" shows the new "Ваш уровень бота". A finished game counts as an exercise of the day. |

### What to judge by feel (please write a line about each)

- Does level 1 feel beatable but not silly? At which level do you start to lose more than
  you win?
- Are the bot's mistakes "human" (a hanging piece, a missed threat) or do they look random?
- One level up after every win: too fast?
- Is the game review understandable with one arrow and one sentence?
