What is new: the start screen is the list of all 32 traps; every trap has a View screen
(step through any line) and a Learn screen (you play your side, the app answers and gives
hints). Nothing is saved yet: progress, unlocking and reviews come in M3.

If the phone still shows the old app, wait for the banner "Доступна новая версия" and press
"Обновить", or open "Диагностика" and check that "Версия" says 0.3.0 and "Сборка" says `m2 @`.

### What to judge by feel (please write a line about each)

- Are the hint texts clear? Is the order "kind of move, then piece, then arrow" right, or is
  the arrow too early or too late?
- Is the pause before the opponent's move (0.65 s) comfortable?
- Eight to ten variants per trap: is going through all of them useful, or should Learn stop
  after the main line and the "opponent did not fall for it" lines?

## Known limits of this stage

- Nothing is saved: closing the app forgets which variants were completed. All traps are open.
  Saving, unlocking by stage, the backward chain and reviews are M3.
- Texts under the lines are general (one per kind of line). The trap files have short notes
  per line, but they are in English and mostly name the source, so they are not shown.
- There is no skip button inside Learn: a variant has to be finished (or left through
  "К ловушке") to reach the next one.
- Checked by the developer in desktop Chrome at phone size (384 px wide) with mouse clicks,
  and by the Playwright smoke test (headless Chromium, 384 x 824, touch events), which covers
  A1, A4, part of A5, A11 to A13, A17 and A18 on every push. Neither is a real phone:
  Samsung Internet, the installed PWA, dragging with a finger and offline are checked only
  by hand.
